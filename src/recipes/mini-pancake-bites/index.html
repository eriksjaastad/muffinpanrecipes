Mini Pancake Bites

Why Muffin Pans?

Fluffy mini pancake bites bake in the muffin pan, so there is no flipping at the stove. Serve with maple syrup, fruit, or your favorite dip.

Prep 5 mins
Cook 12 mins
Yield 24 servings
Difficulty Easy
Calories (approx) 65 per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups Flour
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 1 large Egg
  • 2 tbsp Butter

Instructions

  1. 01Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 02Whisk batter until smooth.
  3. 03Fill mini cups 2/3 full.
  4. 04Add optional toppings.
  5. 05Bake for 12-14 mins.
