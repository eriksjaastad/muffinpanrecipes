Tandoori Chicken Naan Cups

Jump to Behind the Scenes

Why Muffin Pans?

These Tandoori Chicken Naan Cups pack smoky spiced chicken, cooling yogurt, and quick pickled onions into a soft, crisp-edged naan base molded right into the muffin wells. Each round cup holds together as a hand-held Indian-inspired bite with charry flavor and bright, tangy crunch. They're just as good warm from the oven as they are at room temperature for lunches or parties.

Prep 35 mins
Cook 18 mins
Yield 12 servings
Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (trimmed)
  • 1/2 cup plain whole milk yogurt (not Greek)
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 3/4 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (use 1/2 tsp for spicier)
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour (spooned and leveled)
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup plain whole milk yogurt
  • 1/4 cup whole milk
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (such as canola or grapeseed)
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted (for brushing)
  • 1/2 medium red onion, very thinly sliced (about 1/2 cup)
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup plain whole milk yogurt
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh cilantro (plus more for garnish)
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (for drizzling, optional)

Instructions

  1. 01Cut chicken thighs into small 1/2-inch pieces so they'll nest tightly in the cups.
  2. 02In a medium bowl, whisk together 1/2 cup yogurt, lemon juice, garam masala, cumin, smoked paprika, sweet paprika, coriander, turmeric, cayenne, 3/4 tsp salt, ginger, and garlic until smooth.
  3. 03Add chicken pieces, stir to coat thoroughly, cover, and refrigerate at least 30 minutes (up to 4 hours).
  4. 04In a small bowl, combine apple cider vinegar, water, sugar, and 1/4 tsp salt. Stir until sugar dissolves.
  5. 05Add sliced red onion, press down to submerge, and let sit at room temperature while you continue. Stir occasionally.
  6. 06In a medium bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp sugar.
  7. 07In a small bowl, whisk 1/4 cup yogurt, milk, and neutral oil until smooth.
  8. 08Pour wet ingredients into dry and stir with a fork until a shaggy dough forms.
  9. 09Turn onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently for 1 to 2 minutes until smooth and soft, adding a light dusting of flour only if very sticky.
  10. 10Form into a ball, cover with plastic wrap or an inverted bowl, and let rest at room temperature for 15 minutes. This relaxes the gluten so the dough presses cleanly into the round wells.
  11. 11Position a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 400°F.
  12. 12Generously grease a standard 12-cup muffin tin with butter or nonstick spray, making sure to coat the round bottom and sides of each well so the naan cups release cleanly.
  13. 13After resting, divide the dough into 12 equal pieces (about 1 heaping tablespoon each). I weigh them when I'm being fussy, but eyeballing is fine here.
  14. 14Roll each piece into a ball, then flatten into a 3 to 3 1/2-inch round with your fingers or a small rolling pin.
  15. 15Press each round firmly into a muffin well, working it up the sides so you form a neat cup with a thin, even layer of dough all around. The dough should hug the round sides and flare slightly at the top edge.
  16. 16Brush the inside of each dough cup lightly with melted butter (reserve remaining butter for later).
  17. 17Line a baking sheet with foil for easy cleanup. Using tongs, lift chicken pieces from the marinade, letting excess drip off, and spread in a single layer on the sheet. Discard leftover marinade.
  18. 18Bake at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes, just until the chicken is mostly cooked through and starting to get a few browned edges. It will finish cooking inside the naan cups.
  19. 19Remove from oven and reduce oven temperature to 375°F.
  20. 20Spoon the par-cooked chicken pieces evenly into the naan-lined wells, packing them gently so each cup is nicely mounded but not overflowing (about 2 heaping tablespoons per cup).
  21. 21Brush the exposed edges of the naan cups with the remaining melted butter for good color.
  22. 22Bake at 375°F for 13 to 16 minutes, until the naan edges are golden brown and crisp, the bottoms are set, and the chicken registers at least 165°F. You should see the dough clearly holding the round, molded cup shape.
  23. 23While the cups bake, in a small bowl whisk together 1/2 cup yogurt, lemon juice, cilantro, and 1/4 tsp salt until smooth. If you like a thinner drizzle, whisk in 1 to 2 tsp water.
  24. 24Remove muffin pan from the oven and let the cups rest in the pan for 5 minutes so the naan firms up and pulls slightly from the sides.
  25. 25Run a thin knife or offset spatula carefully around the edge of each cup to loosen.
  26. 26Lift each Tandoori Chicken Naan Cup straight up from the round well and transfer to a wire rack or serving plate. They should stand on their own as round, individual cups with crisp, flared edges.
  27. 27Drain the pickled onions.
  28. 28Top each warm naan cup with a small spoonful of yogurt drizzle, a few strands of pickled onion, and a pinch of fresh cilantro.
  29. 29Drizzle lightly with olive oil if desired and serve warm or at room temperature.

Chef's Notes

- The dough should feel soft and slightly tacky, not dry. If it seems stiff when mixing, add milk 1 tsp at a time until it just comes together; that suppleness is what lets it mold cleanly to the round wells without shrinking. - Press the dough thinly and evenly in the wells - too thick at the base and it won't bake through by the time the chicken is done. Aim for about 1/8 inch. - You can cook the chicken under a broiler for extra char before adding to the cups, but watch it closely - those small pieces go from browned to dry in a blink. - These hold well at room temperature for a couple of hours, making them good for buffets. Reheat on a sheet pan at 325°F for 8 to 10 minutes; the naan will re-crisp. - Variations: swap chicken for paneer cubes or chickpeas for a vegetarian version (reduce baking time by a couple of minutes), and adjust cayenne to your heat tolerance.
