Sweet

Dark Chocolate Chip Decadence

Why Muffin Pans?

Rich cocoa muffins loaded with dark chocolate chips bake into individual portions with crisp edges and a tender center. Make a batch for a chocolate snack.

Prep 15 mins
Cook 18 mins
Yield 12 servings
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups Flour
  • 1/2 cup Cocoa
  • 3/4 cup Sour cream
  • 1 cup Choc chips

Instructions

  1. 01Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. 02Sift dry ingredients.
  3. 03Whisk wet ingredients.
  4. 04Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. 05Bake for 18-20 mins.
