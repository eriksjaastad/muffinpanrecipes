Buffalo Chicken Mac & Cheese Bites

Why Muffin Pans?

Buffalo chicken mac and cheese bites combine cheddar, chicken, and spicy sauce in crisped portions. Bake them in a muffin pan for an easy party snack.

Prep 20 mins
Cook 15 mins
Yield 24 servings
Difficulty Medium
Calories (approx) 85 per serving

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Macaroni
  • 2 cups Cheddar
  • 1 cup Chicken
  • 1/4 cup Buffalo sauce

Instructions

  1. 01Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. 02Mix all ingredients.
  3. 03Pack firmly into mini cups.
  4. 04Add panko topping.
  5. 05Bake for 15-18 mins.
