Jumbo Cornbread Honey Bites

Why Muffin Pans?

Jumbo cornbread honey bites are moist, lightly sweet, and perfect with chili or barbecue. The muffin pan gives each portion golden edges.

Prep 10 mins
Cook 22 mins
Yield 6 servings
Difficulty Easy
Calories (approx) 280 per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Cornmeal
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1 cup Buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp Honey

Instructions

  1. 01Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. 02Mix dry ingredients.
  3. 03Whisk wet ingredients.
  4. 04Combine and scoop into jumbo cups.
  5. 05Bake for 22-25 mins.
