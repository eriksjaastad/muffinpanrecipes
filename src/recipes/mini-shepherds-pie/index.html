Savory

Mini Shepherd's Pie Pots

Why Muffin Pans?

Individual Shepherd's pie cups layer savory meat filling beneath a toasted potato peak. The muffin pan turns this comfort food into easy portions.

Prep 25 mins
Cook 20 mins
Yield 12 servings
Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground lamb/beef
  • 3 medium Potatoes
  • 1/2 cup Beef broth
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste

Instructions

  1. 01Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 02Cook meat filling.
  3. 03Mash potatoes.
  4. 04Layer meat and top with potato.
  5. 05Bake until golden.
