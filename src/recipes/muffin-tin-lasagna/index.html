Muffin Tin Lasagna Cups

Why Muffin Pans?

Muffin tin lasagna cups layer crisp wonton wrappers, marinara, ricotta, and mozzarella. Each portion has golden edges and classic Italian comfort.

Prep 20 mins
Cook 20 mins
Yield 12 servings
Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 24 Wonton wrappers
  • 1 3/4 cups Marinara
  • 1 cup Ricotta
  • 1 cup Mozzarella

Instructions

  1. 01Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. 02Layer wontons and ricotta.
  3. 03Add sauce and cheese.
  4. 04Repeat layers.
  5. 05Bake until crispy.
