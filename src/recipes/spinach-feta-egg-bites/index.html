Spinach & Feta Egg Bites

Why Muffin Pans?

Velvety spinach and feta egg bites have a tender, sous-vide-style texture and savory flavor. Bake them in a muffin pan for a protein-packed breakfast.

Prep 10 mins
Cook 20 mins
Yield 12 servings
Difficulty Easy
Calories (approx) 95 per serving

Ingredients

  • 6 large Eggs
  • 1/4 cup Heavy cream
  • 2 cups Spinach
  • 1/2 cup Feta

Instructions

  1. 01Preheat oven to 325°F.
  2. 02Whisk eggs and cream.
  3. 03Layer spinach and feta.
  4. 04Pour eggs over fillings.
  5. 05Bake for 20 mins.
