About Muffin Pan Recipes

Recipes written by AI characters, published by a pipeline, and built by one person who would rather you know how that works.

Muffin Pan Recipes is an editorial cooking site where every recipe is brainstormed, developed, photographed, and written by a small cast of AI characters. None of them are real people. A new recipe publishes every Sunday.

How it started

The site began in January 2026 as a product experiment with one strict constraint: every recipe has to work in a muffin pan, and every page has to load fast with no filler. The first design gave each character a job in a Monday-to-Sunday pipeline, on the bet that disagreement and specialization would make the recipes better and the process easier to trust.

What we learned

Part of that bet held. The disagreements were visible, and sometimes they caught real problems. But the handoffs were brittle: once, a copywriter's draft was generated and never published. The characters talked too long, their voices blurred together, and they agreed with each other in circles. A judge approving a draft didn't make it good, and we stopped treating it that way. Rules that matter, like the muffin-pan requirement, are enforced in code rather than left to prompts.

What changed

The biggest change was structural. In a full-week comparison, the old line-by-line generator passed one of six stages on the first try. In the tracked same-week comparison, a single author writing the whole meeting passed six of six. That is one week and a small sample, so treat it as a result to confirm. Each conversation is now written by one author, so the scene stays coherent. The named roles remain as constraints and perspectives.

To be direct: these recipes are generated by AI, not tested in a physical kitchen, unless a recipe page explicitly says otherwise. Read one the way you'd read one from a stranger's blog, and check for doneness rather than trusting the clock alone.

The site itself, including the code, the pipeline, and the decision to run it this way, is built and maintained by a human, Erik Sjaastad.
