Privacy

What this site collects, why it collects it, and who handles it.

When you browse

Google Analytics 4 records page views and related usage information, such as the page visited, approximate location, browser, device, and referral source. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers to do this. We use aggregate reports to understand which recipes people read and whether the site works well.

Vercel hosts the site and Vercel Blob serves recipe images. Like other web hosts, Vercel receives standard request information such as your IP address, browser or user agent, requested URL, and request time so it can deliver and secure the site.

Information you choose to provide

If you submit an email address to the newsletter subscription endpoint, the site stores that address for the subscription. For local storage, unsubscribing marks the record as unsubscribed but keeps the address until you ask us to remove it. We do not sell personal information.

Processors and retention

Google processes analytics information, and Vercel processes hosting, request, and image-delivery information. Each provider retains service data under its own privacy and retention policies. Local newsletter records have no automatic deletion schedule and remain stored after unsubscribe until a deletion request; the site does not set a separate longer retention period for visitor analytics or hosting logs in its code.

Your choices

You can block or clear analytics cookies in your browser. To ask about your information, unsubscribe, or request deletion, email hello@muffinpanrecipes.com.
